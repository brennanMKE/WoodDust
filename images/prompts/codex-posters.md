# Codex task: Dust Almanac shop posters, round 4 (Shave and Sunbeam)

Hand this whole file to Codex. Generate the two posters below, save each at the exact path, then stop and show both. The prompts are already assembled; paste each one as written. The design source is `docs/posters.md`, and the parts these prompts are built from are in `codex-art.md` §D.

## Rules

- **Attach the character sheet:** `images/art/_character-sheet.png` goes with each prompt.
- **Generate** 1024×1536 portrait on cream, with no transparency. Keep it full size and don't resize it.
- **No text anywhere:** no letters, numbers, signatures, labels or fake writing. Redo any image that has some.
- **Paul:** clean chin and jaw with a short trimmed mustache in every view, including the mirror reflection. His safety gear is worn correctly: respirator straps behind his head and the mask sealed on a clean-shaven face, glasses worn rather than perched.
- **Leave the two empty areas plain:** the slogan area and the blank square surface for the QR code, about one-sixth of the image width and facing the viewer. The layout adds the slogan and the QR code later.
- **Make it original:** nothing that resembles the Bemidji or Brainerd statues, Disney's 1958 *Paul Bunyan*, or any specific vintage poster.
- **Only write** these two files. Don't edit code or docs, commit, push, or touch any server.
- **When done,** print a table with path, pixel size, file size and a pass/redo note.
